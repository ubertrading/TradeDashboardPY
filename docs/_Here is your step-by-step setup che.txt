Here is your step-by-step setup checklist for your Trade Dashboard and your Phone.

Part 1: Trade Dashboard Setup
Open Dashboard Settings:

Go to the Settings tab in the Trade Dashboard UI.
Configure Standard Telegram (Bot 1):

Make sure Bot 1 Token and Chat ID are filled in under 📢 Telegram Notifications.
Under 🌙 Standard Telegram Night Quiet Hours:
Check Enable Quiet Hours.
Set Quiet Start (e.g. 22:00 / 10:00 PM).
Set Quiet End (e.g. 07:00 / 7:00 AM).
Configure Red Alert Channel (Bot 2):

Under 🚨 Red Alert Telegram Channel (High Priority):
Check Enabled.
Enter your Bot 2 Token and Chat ID.
Click Save Settings.
Click 🚨 Test Red Alert to verify the test message arrives on your phone!
Part 2: Phone & Telegram App Setup
Set Up Bot 1 (Standard Alerts):

Open Telegram on your phone and open the chat with Bot 1.
Tap Bot 1’s name at the top > Mute (or leave notification sounds quiet).
(The Dashboard will automatically stop sending messages to Bot 1 during your Night Quiet Hours anyway!)
Set Up Bot 2 (Red Alert Alarm Bot):

Open the chat with Bot 2 in Telegram.
Tap Bot 2’s name at the top > Notifications > Customize:
Sound: Choose a loud, urgent alarm/siren tone (e.g. Beacon, Radar, or Alarm).
Priority: Set to High / Urgent (so it turns on the screen and vibrates aggressively).
Phone Do Not Disturb (DND) / Focus Mode:

In your phone’s Do Not Disturb / Night Focus settings:
Add Telegram to the "Allowed Apps" list.
Because Bot 1 is muted during the night, only Bot 2’s loud alarm will break through DND and ring to wake you up!
(Optional) Read Alerts Out Loud:

Turn on Announce Notifications (iOS: Settings > Siri & Search > Announce Notifications) or Read Aloud (Android) for Telegram so Siri or Google Assistant reads out the exact message out loud (e.g. "Red Alert: Rebalance / Rollback Prompt on HU-01").
